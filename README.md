# Fitness Assistant

A personal 8-week vegetarian fat-loss and muscle-building dashboard.

## Profile
- Age: 31
- Male
- Height: 183 cm / 6 ft
- Starting weight: 95 kg
- Waist: 36 in
- Activity: ~2,000 steps/day
- Training: 4 mornings/week
- Diet: Vegetarian, no eggs; dairy and whey allowed
- Daily calorie target: ~2,100 kcal
- Daily protein target: 150–165 g
- Water target: 2.5–3 L

## Goals
- Primary: lose body fat while building/preserving muscle
- 8-week target range: 87–90 kg
- Stretch target: 85 kg

## MVP
- Daily dashboard
- Check-ins for weight, waist, steps, calories, protein, water, sleep, workout and mood
- Vegetarian meal plan
- 4-day morning gym programme
- Walking progression
- Outside-food / biryani guidance
- Progress charts

This app is informational and not medical advice.
